     F3Supply allows you, as the Squadron Commander, to
     requisition additional supplies of all stores, armament
     and F-16 aircraft for use in your Campaigns.
     It has been developed for use with Falcon 3.0 ver. 3.02
     and it is intended to be just a simple, quick solution to
     your logistic problems.


       INSTALLATION
     ÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄ
     To install F3Supply, just copy the two unarchived files
     to your \falcon3 directory.

     Before you run the program, it's in your own interest to
     backup your Campaign files. I suggest creating a 
     falcon3\squad sub-directory, then copying your files
     there.

     To do this from your dos prompt:

     - make the drive holding Falcon 3.0 current and change to
       the \falcon3 directory.

         `drive':
         cd\falcon3

     - create the new \squad sub-directory.

         md squad

     - copy your campaign files

         copy squad?.* \squad\*.*
         copy mission.sav \squad\*.*
     
     You should backup your campaign files BEFORE you run
     F3Supply, EVERY time you run it, so starting the program
     via a batch file is a good idea.

     When the time comes to delete F3Supply, just zap the two
     files:

       f3supply.exe
       f3supply.txt

     from your \falcon3 directory.
     ÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄ


       RUNNING F3SUPPLY
     ÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄÄ
     Start the program by typing `f3supply' in your \falcon3
     directory, at the dos prompt.

     A list of your Active Squadron's is displayed.
     To select a Squadron to resupply, just type the relevant
     number displayed to the left of the filename.
     F3Supply checks your Squadrons and providing it does not
     detect any errors, the stores will be replenished to the
     following levels:

        F-16:          18

        20mm Ammo:  98970      MK.82:        250
        AIM-9P:       240      MK.83:        200
        AIM-9R:       240      MK.84:        175
	AIM-9M:       200      Durandal:     150
        AIM-120:      175      MK.82HD:      250
        AIM-120E:     175      CBU-84:       200
                               GBU-15:       100
        AGM-65D:      150      Paveway:      n/a
        AGM-84A:       20
        AGM-65B:      150
        LAU 5003-A:   150
        AGM-88A:      100
        AGM-45A:      100
        ALQ-131:       50

        Flares:      5940
        Chaff:       5940
        Fuel lbs.: 450000

     a message is displayed to confirm the resupply.

     Quantities are closely related to the those issued at the
     beginning of a Campaign - just when the enemy thought
     attrition was taking effect!
