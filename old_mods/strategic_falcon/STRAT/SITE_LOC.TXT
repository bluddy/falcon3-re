          Site Locations:  Long Integer Format


These are the "Debug Screen" locations for all the sites in the
first six theaters of Falcon 3.0.  It took me months of frantic
searching to nail them all down (actually they're a by-product
of building StratFalc;  this list was generated in a
couple of seconds).

Bosnia isn't included because I didn't have the data prettied up 
by press time.

I thought they might be of use for exploration or reconnaissance
during campaigns.  To take a look at a site, enter the Debug World.
Here's the procedure in case you're not familiar with
it:

    *   As soon as you land in the F-16 cockpit, press <P>,
        Press <TAB> to enter the Debug world, then press <T> to
        get to the transporter room.

    *   Type in the desired coordinates from the list below.

    *   Press the <~> (tilde key, usually upper-left of the
        keyboard), count to two, and press it again.  You have
        to press this key to have Falcon "register your new
        focus position" and activate the immediate area.  Otherwise,
        the units at the site won't appear.  The second press
        refreezes the action;  if you don't do this, time keeps
        flowing in the Falcon world and your F-16 is without a
        pilot!

The movement keys you can use are <F3>, <F4>, <Shift-F3>,
<Shift-F4>, <Page Up>, <Page Down>, and the arrow keys.  The
numeric keypad <+> key speeds up the rate of movement, and the
numeric keypad <-> key slows it down.
