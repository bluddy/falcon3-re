j Negative. Pigeons to home plate – From AWACS. You have tried to advance your

Warning Indicator.

j Guns! Guns! Guns!– Your wingman is firing his M61A1 cannon.
j Flame out – Your wingman has been hit and has engine damage or is totally out of fuel.

bandit’s direction.
j Singer high! – A SAM has been launched.
j Request you check joker – Your wingman is running out of fuel and wants to let you
know that you should be aware of your own fuel situation.
j Be advised. Fuel is low – Your wingman has only enough fuel to return to base. Any
action other than returning to base could be cause for a flameout.
If you are getting overwhelmed by radio chatter, you can tell everyone to shut up by pressing
sR.

Wingman commands
You can direct your wingmen to take several different actions during a mission.
j sA Tell wingman to break left. A break turn is a maximum G turn into an
attacking bandit. Direct your wingman to break if you detect a bandit at his
6 o’clock closing into a firing position or if a missile is in the air.
j sD Tell wingman to break right.
j sW Tell wingman to break high.
j sX Tell wingman to break low.
j sS Tell wingman to fly straight and level.
j sF Tell all wingmen to close up formation. If you order your wingmen to close
up, they will halve the distance between the planes in the flight.
j sG Tell all wingmen to spread formation. If you order your wingmen to spread
out, they will double the distance between the planes (but not so far as to
lose visual contact).
j sB Bypass current waypoint.
j sV Return to current waypoint.
j sR Tell all wingmen to keep radio silence.
j sZ Tell wingman to bracket left. Usually executed outside of 15 miles, a
bracket is a pincer move to intercept a bandit formation from two sides.
j sC Tell wingman to bracket right.
j sE Tell wingman to engage. If you order your wingman to engage, he will
enter a turning fight with the nearest bandit.
j sQ Tell wingman to disengage.
j s2 Tell wingman to drag left. The drag is a maneuver designed to decoy the
enemy and have him engage a fighter that is leaving the fight.
j s3 Tell wingman to drag right.
j sN Ask AWACS for navigation information.
